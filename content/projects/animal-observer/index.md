A fun, simple animal counting game.

Watch as nature takes its path and these animals ruthlessly fight each other.
As a researcher, it is your job to keep track and collect data. Watch out for
distrations or else you're fired.

- Animals: Cow, Sheep, Wolf, Shark, Ice Cream
- Demonstrates concepts like correlation vs causation and scientific method

This was created for a scientific methods and inquiry class.
